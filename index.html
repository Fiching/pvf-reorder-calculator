How the calculation works

For every stocked item (TIER 1–5), the tool computes a projected stock position and compares it to a target based on your monthly demand.

Target Stock  =  Avg Monthly Demand × (Target Months + Safety Months)
Position  =  On Hand + On PO − On BO
Order Qty  =  MAX(0, Target − Position), rounded up to PPQ

Why this works: On Hand is what's on the shelf. On PO is already coming in from the vendor — you don't want to double-order it. On BO is what's already owed to customers — that stock is effectively already gone. Avg Dmd Amt is your monthly run-rate.

Items excluded by default: Anything flagged NS/TRN/OBS (non-stock, transition, obsolete) in the Tier column — you wouldn't want to replenish items you're phasing out. Change the Tier Filter above if you want to see them.

Rounding: If PPQ is populated (carton/case quantity), the recommended qty rounds up to the nearest multiple. You'll never get a suggestion to buy 17 of something that ships in cases of 50.

UOM handling (pipe is priced per 100): Pipe items carry a UOM of C meaning Avg Cost is the price per 100 feet, not per foot. The tool auto-detects this by cross-checking the system's extended amounts (Ext OHB Amt, Ext Avail Amt, Ext Open PO Amt) against the quantity — so a pipe line showing $921/C correctly reflects as $9.21/ft in the Unit Cost column and the Ext. Cost calculation. The UOM column on the table shows C (per 100), M (per 1,000), or EA for each SKU so you can eyeball it.

Smart Filter (optional): Two rules to keep junk off your buy list:

   • Rule 1 — Slow & cheap: If monthly demand ≤ threshold and unit cost < threshold, skip. Catches the "3 a month at $2 each" items that aren't worth the PO handling cost.

   • Rule 2 — Minimum line value: If the proposed line value (Qty × Unit Cost) is below the threshold, skip. Keeps you from ordering 1 of a $1 widget but preserves 1 of a $100 item.

Rule 1 runs first, then Rule 2. Set Show = "All matched items" to see skipped lines with the reason.
